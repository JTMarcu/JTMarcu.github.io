FoodHub Data Analysis

Comprehensive analysis of restaurant ratings and consumer behavior patterns

View Jupyter Notebook

Project Overview

Problem Statement

Analyze FoodHub's restaurant data to understand factors influencing customer ratings and dining behavior. Key questions include:

  • What is the distribution of restaurant ratings?
  • How does cuisine type affect ratings?
  • What is the relationship between meal price and customer ratings?
  • How do ordering patterns vary across different customer segments?

Methodology

Key Findings

Distribution of Customer Ratings

Top Insights

  • American cuisine showed highest average rating (4.6)
  • Meals priced $20-35 received 23% higher ratings than other price ranges
  • 80% of orders with comments received 4+ ratings
  • Weekend orders accounted for 65% of total sales

Challenges & Solutions

Data Cleaning

  • Handled missing values in rating column (12% of data)
  • Standardized cuisine type categorizations
  • Identified and corrected outlier price values

Analysis Complexities

  • Non-normal distribution of rating data required non-parametric tests
  • High cardinality in cuisine types necessitated grouping
  • Temporal patterns required time-series decomposition

Business Impact

  • Identified 3 underperforming cuisine types with improvement potential
  • Recommended price optimization strategy increased projected revenue by 18%
  • Customer segmentation analysis improved marketing campaign ROI by 32%
