Project Overview

March Madness Prediction Model

This project leverages detailed men's and women's NCAA basketball data to build a robust model that predicts tournament matchups with well-calibrated probabilities. By combining advanced team stats, year-based validation, and thorough hyperparameter tuning, we aim to provide meaningful win probabilities for the 2025 tournament.

Key questions addressed:

  • How can advanced performance metrics (offensive/defensive efficiency, shooting %, etc.) improve predictions?
  • What impact does home/away/neutral location have on outcomes?
  • How do we ensure robust calibration of predicted probabilities?
  • What is the best way to separate training (2010–2023) and validation (2024) to avoid data leakage?

Key Findings

Highlights from Our Analysis

Insights & Model Performance

  • Detailed Data Outperforms Compact Data: Access to rebounds, assists, turnovers, etc., improved feature engineering.
  • Year-Based Validation: Splitting 2010–2023 (train) vs. 2024 (validation) provided realistic performance checks and avoided data leakage across seasons.
  • Home/Away Advantage: Encoding location (H/A/N) as +1/-1/0 added an important predictive signal, especially relevant in regular season contexts.
  • Hyperparameter Tuning Matters: Searching over max_depth, n_estimators, etc., led to better Brier Score compared to default settings.
  • Balanced Labels Boost Calibration: Inverting winner/loser roles effectively doubled the data and balanced outcomes, improving probability predictions.

Challenges & Solutions

Data & Feature Engineering

  • Issue: Division-by-zero in advanced stats caused infinite or NaN values.
  • Solution: Replaced inf with NaN, then imputed using medians to preserve dataset integrity.
  • Issue: Combining men’s & women’s data introduced potential ID collisions.
  • Solution: Verified ID ranges do not overlap, appended data safely.

Year-Based Validation

  • Issue: Random splitting can cause overoptimistic results by leaking future season info.
  • Solution: Training on 2010–2023 & validating on 2024 ensures realistic performance estimates for new seasons like 2025.

Hyperparameter Tuning & Probabilities

  • Issue: Default parameters led to suboptimal calibration (high Brier Score).
  • Solution: Used RandomizedSearchCV optimizing log loss/brier score, improved model’s probability accuracy.

Business Impact

  • Data-Driven Insights: Provided advanced analytics for bracket pools and sports analysis, enabling more confident picks.
  • Improved Probability Calibration: Potentially helps betting markets, bracket challenges, or media coverage with well-founded upset predictions.
  • Scalable Template: The pipeline can generalize to other sports or tournaments requiring advanced stats and year-based validation.
