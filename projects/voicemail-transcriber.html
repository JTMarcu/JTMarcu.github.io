Voicemail & Call Categorizer (Whisper + NLP)

Transform raw voicemails and phone calls into structured, actionable information—automatically transcribe, extract caller details, categorize tasks, and streamline follow-up for your team.

App Demo

Project Overview

The Voicemail & Call Categorizer is a smart, privacy-first Streamlit app for businesses and teams. It transcribes voicemail or phone call audio in seconds, then automatically extracts and organizes key information such as caller location, task/request, name, and phone number. By turning unstructured audio into actionable, categorized data, this tool accelerates client response, enables task prioritization, and lays the groundwork for workflow automation.

  • Multi-format Audio Support: Upload or record voicemails/calls as .wav, .mp3, or .ogg files.
  • Instant Transcription: Leverages OpenAI Whisper to generate high-quality transcripts, locally.
  • Entity Extraction: NLP modules extract location (city/zip), problem/task, caller name, and phone number.
  • Smart Categorization: Messages are tagged for easy routing—by location, urgency, or problem type.
  • Actionable Output: Structured results can feed into dashboards, CRM, or task queues.

Capabilities & Use Cases

  • Automated Call Triage: Route and prioritize calls by location, type of request, or urgency.
  • Client Follow-up: Quickly view caller info and action items in a structured format.
  • Team Collaboration: Distribute tasks to the right team member based on skill or region.
  • Analytics & Reporting: Aggregate call trends, top locations, or most common issues for business insights.

Technical Stack

  • Python 3.11
  • Streamlit (UI, audio handling, dashboard)
  • OpenAI Whisper (ASR, speech-to-text)
  • Custom NLP (spaCy, Regex, transformers for entity extraction)
  • PyTorch (CPU-only, for Whisper model)
  • Future: Integration with CRM, CSV/JSON export, company APIs

Challenges & Solutions

Robust Entity Extraction

  • Used pattern matching and named entity recognition to accurately pull location, phone, and names from real-world transcripts.
  • Addressed edge cases: noisy backgrounds, informal speech, and missing metadata.

Seamless, Secure Workflow

  • All processing is local, respecting privacy and compliance needs.
  • Simple UI makes it easy for non-technical users to process calls and act on them quickly.

Business Impact

  • Faster, more organized client response—no calls lost in the shuffle.
  • Clear routing for task distribution and follow-up, improving team efficiency.
  • Actionable insights for business leaders, supporting smarter resource allocation.
